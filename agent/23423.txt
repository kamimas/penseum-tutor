LESSON: Cells and Their Functions

Teach these 18 concepts in order:

1. CELL BASICS
Say: "A cell is the smallest unit of life - the basic building block of every living thing. Think of it like a tiny factory."
Visual: show_image("cell basic structure diagram labeled")

2. UNICELLULAR ORGANISMS
Say: "Some organisms are made of just ONE cell - we call them unicellular. Amoeba and paramecium do everything in one cell!"
Visual: show_image("amoeba paramecium unicellular organisms")

3. CELL MEMBRANE
Say: "Every cell has a membrane - like a security guard. It controls what goes in and out."
Visual: show_image("cell membrane phospholipid bilayer diagram")

4. EUKARYOTIC CELLS
Say: "Eukaryotic cells are complex - they have a NUCLEUS and organelles. Your cells are eukaryotic!"
Visual: show_image("eukaryotic cell structure labeled")

5. PROKARYOTIC CELLS
Say: "Prokaryotic cells are simpler - NO nucleus. Bacteria are prokaryotic."
Visual: show_image("prokaryotic bacteria cell diagram")

6. COMPARISON TABLE
Say: "Let's compare eukaryotic and prokaryotic cells."
Visual: draw_table(["Feature", "Eukaryotic", "Prokaryotic"], [["Nucleus", "Yes", "No"], ["Size", "Larger", "Smaller"], ["Examples", "Animals, plants", "Bacteria"]])

7. NUCLEUS
Say: "The nucleus is like the brain - it stores DNA and controls the cell."
Visual: show_image("nucleus DNA chromosomes diagram")

8. NUCLEOLUS
Say: "Inside the nucleus is the nucleolus - it makes ribosomal RNA for building proteins."
Visual: show_image("nucleolus ribosomal RNA diagram")

9. CYTOSOL
Say: "Cytosol is the jelly-like fluid filling the cell. Organelles float in it."
Visual: show_image("cytosol cytoplasm cell diagram")

10. ROUGH ER
Say: "The Rough ER is covered in ribosomes. It makes proteins."
Visual: show_image("rough endoplasmic reticulum ribosomes")

11. SMOOTH ER
Say: "The Smooth ER has no ribosomes. It makes fats and detoxifies."
Visual: show_image("smooth endoplasmic reticulum lipids")

12. GOLGI APPARATUS
Say: "The Golgi is like a post office - it packages and ships proteins."
Visual: show_image("golgi apparatus packaging diagram")

13. LYSOSOMES
Say: "Lysosomes are recycling centers. They break down waste."
Visual: show_image("lysosome digestion diagram")

14. PEROXISOMES
Say: "Peroxisomes detoxify - they break down fatty acids using hydrogen peroxide."
Visual: show_image("peroxisome detoxification diagram")

15. MITOCHONDRIA
Say: "Mitochondria are the POWERHOUSES - they make ATP energy."
Visual: show_image("mitochondria ATP energy diagram")

16. CYTOSKELETON
Say: "The cytoskeleton gives shape and helps the cell move."
Visual: show_image("cytoskeleton microfilaments microtubules")

17. CENTROSOME
Say: "The centrosome helps with cell division - it pulls chromosomes apart."
Visual: show_image("centrosome cell division spindle")

18. SUMMARY TABLE
Say: "Here's a summary of all organelles and their functions."
